Text:
By Peter Fuller

Artists are not supposed to be into sport, but mountain biking is a huge part of my life. It gets me out of my studio into the countryside. But more importantly, racing leaves you no time to worry about anything else - you're too busy concentrating on not crashing.
I started cycling as a kid. My friends and I rode our bikes and were brave enough to go down steep hills. Our bikes weren't built for that, and often broke, so I drew pictures of strong bikes with big tyres.
Then I did skateboarding for a decade until falling off on to hard surfaces started to hurt too much. Mountain biking seemed a fairly safe sport, so I took that up instead. I made a lot of friends, and got involved in racing.
In the end I stopped racing, mainly because I knew what a bad crash could mean to my career, but I still ride a mountain bike every week.
