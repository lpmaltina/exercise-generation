Step 1. Read the text: {text}
- Identify all verb forms in the text (main verbs, auxiliaries, modals, participles, infinitives, gerunds).
- Determine the text's CEFR level based on vocabulary and sentence complexity.
- Note which verb forms are likely target grammar for that level.

Step 2. Select {n_gaps} verb forms to blank out:
- Target verb forms that are likely target grammar for the text's CEFR level.
- Ensure the surrounding context makes the required verb form deducible.

Step 3. Determine correct answers for each gap:
- For each gap, identify the correct verb form.
- Think: Do 2-3 equally valid verb forms of the same lemma exist for this gap?". If yes, include them all in the correct_answers array.
- If the gap has more than 3 possible answers, discard that gap and select a different verb form from the text.

Step 4. Construct the text_with_gaps:
- Replace each chosen verb form with the placeholder "(<gap number>) _____ (<lemma>)".
- Gap ids start from 1 and increase sequentially.
