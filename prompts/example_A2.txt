Text:
By Anna Gray

I went to primary school until I was eleven. Then I had to go to a different school for older children. I loved my primary school but I was excited to move to a new school.

Most of the children were from different schools. But I soon started talking to the girl who was sitting beside me in maths. She lives near me so we walked home together. We're best friends now.

The worst thing is that I have more homework now. Some of it is fun but I need to get better at remembering when I have to give different pieces of work to the teachers!

Questions:
Q1: How did Anna feel about moving to a new school?
Correct: Happy about the idea of doing something different.
Distractors: Worried about being with lots of older children. / Pleased because she was bored at primary school. / Sad because she had good friends at her primary school.
Explanation: Explanation: The text says, "I loved my primary school, but I was excited to move to a new school." The correct answer is "Happy about the idea of doing something different." The option "Pleased because she was bored at primary school" is false because Anna loved her primary school. "Sad because she had good friends at her primary school" is wrong because the text says nothing about friends from her primary school. "Worried about being with lots of older children" is also wrong because the text does not mention being worried.

Q3: What does Anna say about the homework now?
Correct: She thinks it is the hardest part of school life.
Distractors: She gets more help from some teachers than others. / She remembers everything she's told to do. / She has little homework.
Explanation: The text says, "The worst thing is that I have more homework now." The correct answer is "She thinks it is the hardest part of school life." There is nothing in the text about help from teachers. The option "She remembers everything she's told to do" is wrong because Anna writes, "I need to get better at remembering when I have to give different pieces of work to the teachers."
