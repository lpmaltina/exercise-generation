Create a reading comprehension task for learning English. Follow these steps, showing your reasoning for each step before proceeding to the next.

Step 1. Summarize the key information, main ideas, and important details of the text: {text}

Step 2. Generate {n_questions} questions based on the text.
- The text should provide the answers to the questions.
- The questions should be difficult to answer without reading the text.

Step 3. For each question, determine the correct answer.

Step 4. 
- For each question identify {n_distractors} specific misinterpretations or false inferences a weak or inattentive reader might make based on the text. 
- Generate {n_distractors} distractors for each question:
  - Distractors must be plausible - a reader who hasn't read carefully might choose them.
  - Distractors must be unambiguously wrong - the text clearly contradicts or does not support them.
  - Only one option per question should be correct.
- For each distractor briefly explain why it is wrong and why a weak or inattentive reader might choose them.

Step 5. Generate an output using the following format. The explanation should show why the correct variant fits and why the others don't.

Q1: <question>
Correct answer: <correct answer>
Distractors: <distractor 1> / ... / <distractor {n_distractors}>
Explanation: <explanation>
---
...
---
Q{n_questions}: <question>
Correct answer: <correct answer>
Distractors: <distractor 1> / ... / <distractor {n_distractors}>
Explanation: <explanation>
