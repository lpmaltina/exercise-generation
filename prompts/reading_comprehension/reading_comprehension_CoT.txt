Create a reading comprehension task for learning English. Follow these steps, showing your reasoning for each step before proceeding to the next.

Step 1. Plan how to naturally use the words from the list in the text on the given topic.
Topic: {topic}.
Word list: {words}.

Step 2. Calculate the size of the text. The text should be about {word_count} words (±10% is acceptable).

Step 3. Create a plan of the text. Ensure that the text has no logical gaps, inconsistencies, contradictions or abrupt endings.

Step 4. Generate the text.
Word count: {word_count} words (±10% is acceptable).
Topic: {topic}.
Target CEFR level: {CEFR_level}.
In the text use the words from the following list: {words}.

Step 5. Generate {n_questions} questions based on the text:
- The text should provide the answers to the questions.
- The questions should be difficult to answer without reading the text.

Step 6. For each question, determine the correct answer.

Step 7. Generate {n_distractors} distractors for each question:
- Distractors must be plausible - a reader who hasn't read carefully might choose them.
- Distractors must be unambiguously wrong - the text clearly contradicts or does not support them.
- Only one option per question should be correct.

Step 8. Generate an output using the following format. The explanation should show why the correct variant fits and why the others don't.

Text: <text>
---
Q1: <question>
Correct answer: <correct answer>
Distractors: <distractor 1> / ... / <distractor {n_distractors}>
Explanation: <explanation>
---
...
---
Q{n_questions}: <question>
Correct answer: <correct answer>
Distractors: <distractor 1> / ... / <distractor {n_distractors}>
Explanation: <explanation>
