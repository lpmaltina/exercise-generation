Use the scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is very poor and 5 is excellent.
For Reading Dependency 1 is easy to guess without the text and 5 is hard to quess without the text.

Part 1. Evaluate the text.
- Matching the Topic. Does the text match the topic "{topic}"?
- Logic & Commonsense. Is the text logical and consistent with commonsense?
- Vocabulary & Grammar. Is the text grammatically correct and well-worded?

Part 2. Evaluate the questions.

a) Evaluate each question using the following criteria.
- Logic & Commonsense. Is the question logical and consistent with commonsense?
- Vocabulary & Grammar. Is the question grammatically correct and well-worded?
- Text-Based Answerability. Can the question be answered using only the information provided in the text?
- Reading Dependency. How difficult would it be to guess the correct answer without reading the text?
- Answer Unambiguity. Is exactly one answer unambiguously correct based on the text?
- Distractor Plausibility. Are all incorrect options believable to someone who has read the text but not fully understood it?
- Using Paraphrases. Is the correct answer formulated so that it does not match the text verbatim?
- Explanation Quality. Does the explanation accurately describe why the correct answer is right and why the distractors are wrong?

b) Evaluate the questions overall using the following criteria.
- Text coverage. Do the questions cover different parts of the text and are not concentrated in one part?
