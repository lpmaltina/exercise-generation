Topic: a story about an unusual holiday trip

Text: 
Last summer, Lisa planned a very unusual holiday trip. Her friends thought she was lazy for not choosing a beach resort, but Lisa was creative. She booked a train to a small village in the mountains, famous for its attractive but strange traditions. The locals were very polite, and they welcomed her with a big smile.

On the first day, Lisa felt nervous. The village had no tourists, and everything seemed different. She met an old man who gave her a map. "Follow the red line," he said. Lisa was scared at first, but she trusted him. She walked through forests and saw a hidden waterfall. When she arrived, she was surprised to find a group of children building a bridge. "You’re intelligent to visit us!" one boy said. Lisa laughed and helped them. By the end of her trip, she wasn’t worried anymore—she had made new friends and discovered something amazing.

Questions: 
Q1: Why did Lisa’s friends think she was lazy?
Correct: She didn’t choose a beach resort for her holiday.
Distractors: She spent too much time planning the trip. / She didn’t invite them to go with her. / She canceled her trip at the last minute.
Explanation: The text states that Lisa’s friends thought she was lazy because she didn’t pick a typical beach holiday, which is a common and relaxing choice. The other options are not mentioned and contradict the idea that she planned something different (creative) rather than canceling or excluding friends.
---
Q2: What did Lisa feel when she first arrived in the village?
Correct: She was nervous.
Distractors: She was excited. / She was bored. / She was angry.
Explanation: The text explicitly says Lisa felt nervous upon arrival because the village was unfamiliar and unlike tourist destinations. "Excited," "bored," and "angry" are not supported by the text, which highlights her uncertainty and the village’s unusual nature.
---
Q3: What did Lisa do to help the children in the village?
Correct: She helped them build a bridge.
Distractors: She taught them English. / She gave them money. / She showed them how to use a map.
Explanation: The text describes Lisa seeing children building a bridge and then joining them to help. The other options (teaching English, giving money, showing how to use a map) are not mentioned and do not align with the scene of collaborative bridge-building.
