Text: Lena was intelligent and creative, so she decided to plan a holiday trip for her family. Instead of a boring beach holiday, she chose a trip to a small village in the mountains. When they arrived home, her parents were surprised by all the photos.

"Next time, let’s do something normal," her father joked. Lena smiled—she knew they’d had the best holiday ever.
