Text: 
Last summer my friend Mark and I planned a holiday to a strange village in the mountains. I was nervous because no one knew much about it. Mark was lazy and just wanted to relax. When we arrived, the village was attractive. It had colorful buildings and a river full of fish. I was scared at first because the people spoke a different language, but they were very polite. Mark was worried about the weather, but the rain soon stopped. Then we met an intelligent old woman. She was very creative and showed us how to make lanterns from flowers. We were surprised by how much we learned!
---
Q1: Why was the traveler nervous at first?
Correct: They knew little about the village.
Distractors:
1. The weather was too hot. / 2. They got lost. / 3. The food was strange.
Explanation:
The author was nervous because they didn't know much about the village. There is nothing in the text about hot weather, getting lost or strange food.
---
Q2: What is true about the village?
Correct: It is a beautiful mountain village with bright houses.
Distractors:
1. It is a small sea village near the beach. / 2. It is a nice village near the lake. / 3. It is a poor village where it rains very often.
Explanation:
The text describes the village as "a strange village in the mountains". "The village was attractive. It had colorful buildings and a river full of fish". The text says nothing about the sea, the beach and the lake. We don't know if the village is poor. 
---
Q3: How did the old woman help them?
Correct: She showed them how to create beautiful things using flowers.
Distractors:
1. She showed them the way. / 2. She cooked a traditional dinner. / 3. She told them about the history of the village.
Explanation:
The text says: "She showed us how to make creative lanterns from flowers". There is no information about showing the way, cooking a dinner and the history of the village.
